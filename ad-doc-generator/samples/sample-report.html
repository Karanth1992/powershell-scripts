Architecture Narrative

This is a single-domain forest (corp.fabrikam-sample.test) with two sites, HQ-Site and Branch-Site, linked by one site link.

There are two external trust relationships: a bidirectional forest trust with partner.fabrikam-sample.test, and an inbound-only external trust from legacy-domain.fabrikam-sample.test.

All five FSMO roles are held by a single domain controller, DC01, which is a single point of failure for schema, naming, RID, PDC, and infrastructure operations.

DC03 is a read-only domain controller that is not assigned to any site, which will affect client referral behavior for that location.
